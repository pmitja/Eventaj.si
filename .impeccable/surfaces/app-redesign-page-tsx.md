# /redesign — home page redesign preview

Scope: full home page at /redesign with its own header and footer (preview of a replacement visual world; the live / stays untouched). Mode: Persuade.

Audience: Slovenian hosts of birthday parties and weddings (largest volume), plus December company year-end parties. Action: send an inquiry (date + occasion pre-filled into the existing InquiryDialog) or check prices. Proof: real guest photos, 360° clips, 5,0 ★ / 13 Google reviews, 100+ events, published prices. Constraint: keep every content block, link and working form of the current home page; logo.svg binding; noindex.

History: the first direction (raspberry sequin wall, Bagel Fat One) was rejected by the owner as not elegant. Replacement brief from the owner: "elegantno in premium", references from Mobbin.

## Direction contract

THESIS: An evening invitation. The page reads like the printed card for a black-tie party: real night photography edge to edge, a high-contrast didone serif, black and porcelain with one champagne action. Premium comes from restraint, space and the guests' own photos, never from ornament.

OWN-WORLD: Warm night black #0E0D0C and porcelain #F4F2EE (not the live site's cream), stone greys for secondary text, hairline rules instead of cards and shadows. Display in Bodoni Moda (optical-size didone, italic for the emotional word); UI and body in Jost (geometric, Art Deco cousin of the didone). Champagne #CDB17A is reserved for the primary action and the active state. Square-shouldered buttons (2px radius), uppercase tracked button labels. Photos are flat, full-bleed or in strict grids; no tilts, pins or frames.

STORY: Visitor sees a real Gatsby-style party at night and one sentence; picks date + occasion in the booking bar and sends an inquiry. Below, the two booths as an editorial spread with prices, the QR gallery and equipment, the party year, three steps, a dark reference wall, one large review, a calm price board, FAQ, and a closing candlelit album photo.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed gatsby-party photo with a left/bottom night scrim. Header on the photo: logo + wordmark left, nav centre-right, phone, champagne inquiry button. Left: H1 "Spomini, ki trajajo dlje kot zabava" in Bodoni Moda (max 6rem), lede, then a slim translucent booking bar (date, occasion, champagne "Preveri termin"). Proof line under it with hairline dividers.

FORM: Mobbin references — Selfridges editorial hero (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/996475f7-662d-422b-982a-6396723e8404), ETERNO photography serif-over-photo (https://mobbin.com/screens/c47c522d-758e-4549-83cd-2319378b2442), FARFETCH split editorial (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/9e799497-35e0-4e74-9c6f-b8d2dd73587e), Fresha booking pill (https://mobbin.com/screens/9dd18f7e-0f22-4234-8933-4910bc9a796e), Siena Film Foundation dark gallery (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/1115875b-e6af-4b8f-b002-d5aa10ec282e), Oku / Unify serif pricing (https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/f66ba39e-9d4f-4336-955d-8721fef92d47, https://mobbin.com/sites/sections/38e80f31-c10c-4125-bfee-59225009e65e).

SIGNATURE INTERACTION: One authored motion moment: on load the hero photograph settles from a slow 1.06 zoom to rest while the headline fades up. Reduced motion shows it at rest.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
